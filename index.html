The estimate becomes a measurement.

Our models segment the tissue, detect each cell and classify it by its staining. On this Ki-67 image, Cell Analytics classified 87,844 nuclei; the positive ones are shown in magenta.

A proportion that is often judged by eye becomes a number the pathologist can check, correct and repeat.

Two tools, one method.

  • Cell Analytics

    Tissue segmentation, cell detection and quantitative counting in H&E and immunohistochemistry images, for biomarkers such as Ki-67.

  • TIL Analytics

    Quantification of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes, starting with triple-negative breast cancer, with spatial maps of tumor, stroma and lymphocytes for review.
